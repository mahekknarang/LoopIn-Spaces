A QUICK GUIDE

How it
works.

Three steps to a room, with a little DSA working behind the scenes.

01

See which rooms are free

Choose a date and scan the room schedule. Green is open, red is a class, yellow is a pending request, and purple is an approved booking.

02

Send a request

Select an open time, add your details, and keep the Request ID we give you. Pending requests don’t reserve a room.

03

Get the decision

An admin reviews requests in order. After approval, check the result any time with your Request ID.

BEHIND THE SCENES

DSA makes it fast and fair.

Hash map · quick lookup

Indexes venues, lectures, and bookings by their keys.
Used to check availability in the room grid.

Queue · first come, first served

A hand-built linked queue keeps pending requests in arrival order.
Admins review the oldest request first.

Merge sort · clear ordering

Our own merge sort orders venues, time slots, and history.
Search puts smaller suitable rooms first.

Sliding window · consecutive time

Moves across free slots to find a continuous block.
The missing lunch slot breaks a window automatically.

Greedy best fit · save larger rooms

Chooses the smallest room that fits the group and hours.
That keeps larger rooms open for larger events.

Trie · room name search

A prefix tree matches a room’s first letters as you type.
Used by the search box on the booking screen.
